### Building the fan base

Thomas Caldwell — The Oklahoman asked whether the Bisons would compete with existing loyalties to the Cowboys, Chiefs, OU, and Oklahoma State.

Bryan explained that having a professional team nearby can gradually transform someone's allegiance.

His example was a fan initially supporting another NFL franchise but attending more Bisons games because Oklahoma City is considerably closer than Dallas.

Bryan also described his personal experience:

- Grew up in Seattle and experienced the loss of the SuperSonics.
- Moved to Oklahoma City in April 2024.
- Followed the Thunder's 2024 and 2025 playoff runs.
- By the 2026 season, watched almost every Thunder game.
- Recognized the irony of a Seattle native becoming a Thunder fan.

Bryan emphasized that he does not expect fans to abandon their existing teams immediately.

Regarding his own NFL loyalties, he confirmed that the Colts/Bisons are now his priority, while the Seahawks will always hold a special place in his heart.

Bobby Wagner and Pete Carroll reacted warmly to that statement.
