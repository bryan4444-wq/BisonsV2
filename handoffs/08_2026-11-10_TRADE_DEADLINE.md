## 8. The Schneider incident — "Five Seconds Too Late"

The final moments of the trade deadline produced one of the funniest front-office moments of Bryan's ownership so far.

At approximately 3:57 PM EST, Seahawks GM John Schneider called Indianapolis offering to discuss a veteran defensive tackle.

There was one problem.

Scot pulled up Seattle's roster and discovered that the Seahawks had only three defensive tackles:

- Byron Murphy II — 85 OVR, age 23.
- Leonard "Big Cat" Williams — 83 OVR, age 32.
- Mike Morris — 67 OVR, age 25.

Schneider's vague pitch did not initially identify Williams as available. The call ended without a trade.

Bryan then remarked, disappointed:

> "But I'd have traded for Big Cat."

With seconds remaining, Bryan called Schneider back and immediately offered a third-round pick for Leonard Williams.

Schneider verbally agreed to 2027 third-round pick #69 for Williams, but the subsequent call-log audit established that his acceptance came at 4:00:05 PM EST — five seconds after the trade deadline.

The trade was invalid. Williams remains in Seattle, and Indianapolis retains pick #69.

Bryan jokingly defended himself afterward:

> "Hey, he called me at 3:55 saying the wrong stuff. If he wanted to shop Leo, I'd have gotten it done!"

The war room erupted in laughter. Shane was relieved that the deadline had finally ended; Pete was devastated that Big Cat wasn't coming to Indianapolis.

This incident is established as a humorous early Luong-era front-office story. It does not create an outstanding trade agreement or an automatic future acquisition.
