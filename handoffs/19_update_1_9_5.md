### Naming and location decisions

Jonathan initially proposes Northstar Technologies, which Bryan rejects as overly generic. Jonathan subsequently proposes Lạc Việt Technologies, which Bryan rejects because of pronunciation and potential assumptions that the business is based in Vietnam.

Jonathan briefly proposes Veyron Technologies, which Rebecca flags for its association with the Bugatti Veyron.

Bryan ultimately proposes Syntergy Technologies. Jonathan likes the name; Rebecca will investigate trademark conflicts, registrations, and domain availability. It is the working name, not yet a legally cleared identity.

Bryan recommends Seattle for headquarters because Jonathan has led Ascend's Seattle office and already has an established professional network there. Jonathan agrees. An Oklahoma City office remains a potential future expansion, not a current commitment.
