Bryan Luong completes Irsay buyout

December 31, 2026

The $2.04 billion deal brings Luong’s stake to 81%.

The Oklahoma investor syndicate holds the remaining 19%. The Irsays’ ownership and voting rights ended at closing. Carlie Irsay-Gordon’s remaining role is ceremonial for the Indianapolis farewell.

Luong remains Owner and GM. Bobby Wagner and Shane Steichen report directly to him. Sarah Mitchell leads the business side as President of Business Operations.
