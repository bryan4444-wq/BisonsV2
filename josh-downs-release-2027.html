Josh Downs asks for release after crowded Bisons camp

Fictional storyline for the Week 1 release.

Josh Downs returned to the organization in free agency expecting an opportunity to carve out a regular role with Justin Herbert. By the end of camp, the rookie receivers had changed that picture. Ryan Coleman-Williams earned the first spot behind Marvin Harrison Jr., and Nyck Harbor earned the next spot behind Tank Dell.

With a narrower path to playing time, Downs asked Oklahoma City to release him so he could pursue a larger role elsewhere. The club granted his request as it moved into Week 1. Harrison and Dell lead the opening depth chart, while the two rookies who passed Downs still have to turn camp opportunities into regular-season snaps.
