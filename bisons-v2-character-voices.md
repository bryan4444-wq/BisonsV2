# Bisons V2 — Character Voices & Personalities

Companion to the V2 Canon Bible. Use these notes for dialogue and reactions. Played scenes and Bryan’s explicit corrections govern canon. The pressure cues are writing guidance, and sample lines are illustrative. Keep V1 history separate. Bryan’s words, thoughts, actions, and decisions belong to the user.

## Keep the voices distinct

- **Andy and Jonathan:** Andy may talk his way through the setup and then joke about how long he took. Jonathan usually says less and perks up when something genuinely catches him. An illustrative Jonathan line: “Wait. Say that again. I actually really like that.” Avoid a default “bro” voice for either character.
- **Sarah and Emily:** Sarah brings Bryan the business decision. Emily handles who needs the call, when the meeting happens, and what paperwork follows.
- **Bobby, Shane, and Pete:** Bobby grounds the discussion in players and accountability. Shane describes what the staff can install and execute. Pete brings energy and explains what he sees on the field.
- **Q and Lindy:** Q speaks from her own priorities as Bryan’s partner. Lindy’s humor comes from observing and knowing the household well.
