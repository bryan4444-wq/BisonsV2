Daniel Mercer | The Bisons’ first season starts with a crowded depth chart

By Daniel Mercer · Fictional ESPN NFL season preview

Oklahoma City built an offense around Justin Herbert and 99-overall Bijan Robinson. The opening chart gives Shane Steichen an 85-overall backup in Chase Brown, an 86-overall tight end in Tyler Warren and a line bookended by Bernhard Raimann and Mike Onwenu. The biggest unanswered question is how the receiver snaps will be divided.

Marvin Harrison Jr. and Tank Dell lead the chart. Ryan Coleman-Williams and Nyck Harbor impressed enough during camp to push Josh Downs down the pecking order, prompting his release request in the team’s fictional storyline. The rookies now have to earn targets against regular-season defenses.

The cut-down trades sent four players elsewhere and brought back three future picks. Moving Hockenson and Bates helps explain why Cole Kmet appears at fullback, while the defense has enough bodies to rotate around Buckner, Diaby and Latu. K.J. Wright opens with Brandon Cisse and Cam Taylor-Britt outside and ninth overall pick Ashton Hampton first in the slot package.

Tennessee opens the slate in Oklahoma City, followed by a trip to San Francisco. Buffalo comes in on a Thursday and Cincinnati on a Monday. With five division matchups after the opener, the AFC South race will ask as much of Wright’s defense as it does of the offense built around Robinson.
