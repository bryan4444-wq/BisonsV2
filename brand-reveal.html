Oklahoma City

The Bisons identity takes the stage

December 1, 2026

Oklahoma City unveiled its name, primary mark and orange-and-blue colors at Devon Tower.

The league approved the name, logo and colors ahead of the public unveiling. Uniform approval remains pending.

The club is scheduled to begin play in Oklahoma City in 2027, with Gaylord Family–Oklahoma Memorial Stadium in Norman as the interim home.
