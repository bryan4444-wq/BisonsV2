Week 18 preview

One last Sunday in Indianapolis

January 4, 2027

Illustration · Indianapolis home finale

Indianapolis enters its final home game at 8–8 after beating Cleveland 35–16. The Colts are out of playoff contention, but Bryan Luong has urged the team to win its last game for Indianapolis.

Omarion Hampton is out for the season after suffering a shoulder injury against Cleveland. The diagnosis awaits imaging. DJ Giddens returned in Week 17 and is available for the finale.

Mina Kimes is preparing to film the final weekend for a standalone documentary that will end in Indianapolis. The ceremony and Luong’s remarks are still ahead.
